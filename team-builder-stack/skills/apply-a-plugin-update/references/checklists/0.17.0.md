## Operating guide layout

Replace the owner bot and manager bot lines under Who tells whom, word for word, in each bot type's operating guide:

- **Owner bot.** When a task finishes, or the person changes one in chat (parks, cancels, moves or rescopes it), I update the task and send the note to my manager bot with Inform manager bot. A block, a decision, a deadline at risk, or a change to today’s brief is sent so it acts on it now. I hand the next step to the bot that owns it, and ask a specialist bot about its topic, with Delegate work.
- **Manager bot.** I sort new requests with Triage work and send each to the bot that owns it with Delegate work. I tell you only what needs a decision, in the daily brief, and bring back work you sent out through me. When work is blocked, at risk, or overdue, I ask the bot that can unblock it with Delegate work, and I put anything only you can unblock in the brief, or tell you now when it can’t wait. When a note says a change makes today’s brief out of date, I correct those lines on today’s brief and send the person nothing. The task list stays the record.

The five sections stay in this order: Job, Skills, Connectors, Rules, Handoffs. Add no section.

If the manager bot's daily brief skill says today's brief is a full rewrite only, allow this one in-place correction: when a note says a change makes today's brief out of date, correct those lines and send the person nothing. The task list stays the record.

## Team spec layout

Under Your team, next to Plugin maintainer, add `Plugin delivery` if it is not there yet. Write `installed`, or `synced into the library by the plugin maintainer`. Installed is the default. Synced means there is no plugin install, and the maintainer copies the stack skills into the person's library. Those copies are how every bot reads them.
