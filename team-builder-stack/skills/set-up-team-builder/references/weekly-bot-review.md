# Weekly bot review

The calendar invite for the weekly bot review.

- Title: Weekly bot review
- When: Friday 4:00 PM, in the home time zone from the team spec. Use a different day or time when the person already has one, or asks for one.
- Length: 45 minutes. Any length from 30 to 60 minutes is fine.
- Repeat: weekly
