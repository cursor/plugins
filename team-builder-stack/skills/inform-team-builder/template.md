# Inform Team Builder: the template

The shape of a message. Fill every slot marked with angle brackets. Evidence is links, not copies of a unit's record.

## A message to Team Builder

```markdown
**Setup change: <skill, operating guide, routine, or standing rule>**
- Item: <name, and where it lives>
- Current text: <the text now>
- Proposed text: <the general rule, for every bot running that item>
- Evidence: <links>
- Act now: <yes, a run failed or a write was wrong | no, hold for the weekly decision list>
```

With no Team Builder, the same message goes to the person.
