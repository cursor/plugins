# Default rubrics

Start from the rubric that fits the draft, then agree it with the person: keep 4 to 7 points, in their words. Each reviewer scores each point from 1 to 5, with a reason tied to a section of the draft.

## A customer deck

1. The value is stated in the customer's own terms, with numbers from their records.
2. The ask is clear, and the decision maker can say yes to it.
3. Every claim has a source the customer would accept.
4. It fits the time the meeting has.
5. The next steps each have an owner and a date.

## A brief or plan for your team

1. The problem and why it matters now are clear in the first paragraph.
2. The recommendation is one choice, with the options it beat.
3. The cost, the risk, and who does the work are stated.
4. The facts and numbers trace to their sources.
5. The decision being asked for, and by when, is explicit.

## A customer message

1. The point is in the first two lines.
2. It answers what the customer actually asked.
3. The tone fits the relationship and the writing voice.
4. Every promise is one the team can keep, within the compliance guidelines.
5. The next step is clear.

## Anything else

1. It does what its purpose says.
2. The person reading it can act on it without asking a question.
3. Every fact traces to a source.
4. Nothing in it belongs to another unit.
