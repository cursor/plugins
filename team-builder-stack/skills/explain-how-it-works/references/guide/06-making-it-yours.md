# 6. Making it yours

The Team Builder Stack starts from the principles and learns you as you go. Nothing here changes a skill, a bot, or your team spec without your yes.

- **Set up Team Builder.** Your primary bot runs [Set up Team Builder](../../../set-up-team-builder/SKILL.md) when you ask to set up Team Builder or the Team Builder Stack. It shows the about line, profile facts, and the one routine the plugin carries, and on your yes creates one bot named Team Builder. That bot creates Weekly review prep in its own chat. If Team Builder already exists, it asks that bot to run Apply a plugin update, in its own chat.
- **Coach your bots.** Team Builder sets you up, then you improve your bots from your own feedback. When a bot gets something wrong, or a tool, a rule, or a unit changes, run [Coach my bots](../../../coach-my-bots/SKILL.md). It finds the one place that holds what changed and proposes the smallest edit, and tries a rule meant for every bot on one bot first. A skill leads with the outcome: one or two sentences on what the finished work does for its reader. Then a few habits, each one line, then one or two worked examples from your real work. A ban goes in only where a mistake would be costly or can't be undone, and each ban says why. Ask it to find work worth a skill, and it looks through the recent chats you allow.
- **Retire a bot.** When a bot or a group chat is replaced or no longer needed, [Retire a bot](../../../retire-a-bot/SKILL.md) moves what it knew into your systems of record, finds everything that still points to it, and keeps it paused until its replacement runs cleanly. You do the deleting.
- **Capture what a session taught you.** [Save what we learned](../../../save-what-we-learned/SKILL.md) reviews the conversation and proposes edits to the skills involved. Another bot sends those edits to Team Builder with Inform Team Builder. When Team Builder runs it, it applies only the edits you pick.
- **Keep your voice current.** [Set my writing voice](../../../set-my-writing-voice/SKILL.md) writes your writing voice skill from your own writing, and updates it when your voice changes.
- **Check how it's all set up.** [Check my bots](../../../check-my-bots/SKILL.md) learns how you use your bots, compares them with your team spec and the principles, refreshes your team map after a yes, and proposes fixes without changing anything else.
- **Let it keep watch.** [Set up Team Builder](../../../set-up-team-builder/SKILL.md) turns on Weekly review prep, the one routine, two hours before the weekly bot review. The scheduled run drafts the review pack to a dated file and posts the summary and the Needs you items in your chat with Team Builder before the meeting. That draft checks the installed plugin version against the team spec, runs the full Check my bots pass, and gathers skill notes. Asking bots for memory reports happens when you reply to the summary in your chat with Team Builder. Applying changes happens once you say yes in that chat. Prep also looks for a missing bot and puts any proposal on the decision list. [Check my bots](../../../check-my-bots/SKILL.md) stays available whenever you ask, including its spot check.
- **Ask why.** [Explain how it works](../../../explain-how-it-works/SKILL.md) answers questions about the Team Builder Stack from the principles file and this guide, and names the part it used.
