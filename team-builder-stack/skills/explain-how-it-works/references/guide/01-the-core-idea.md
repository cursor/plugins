# 1. The core idea

> Find what carries its own history and give each one a bot. Turn each repeated workflow into a skill. Write everything to a record, keep the decisions with people, and check with fresh eyes.

Give a bot to anything that carries its own history. Everything else is a skill.

## When something gets its own bot

- **It carries its own history.** Its past makes the next piece of work better.
- **It lasts.** It runs about a month or longer.
- **You’d lose something if it were rebuilt from the record alone.** It holds judgment that isn’t written down anywhere.
- **Scope of work.** Size the unit to the person’s scope of work. A handful of strategic accounts each get their own bot. With 150 renewals, the renewal book is the unit, one owner bot owns it, and each renewal is an item it works through.

Otherwise it’s a skill or an item. Short tasks, one-off requests, and items in a queue run as skills on an existing bot. A topic whose know-how every unit uses, like product knowledge, is the exception. It gets a specialist bot.

## The build order

1. **Decide who owns what.** Start from outcomes: what the person is accountable for, meaning what their leader judges them on, not their task list. Find the units that carry their own history, such as accounts, projects, or roles, and size each one to the person’s scope of work. A queue or a book of work is one unit one owner bot owns.
2. **Connect your systems.** Give each bot the systems it needs, and pick the record where each workflow’s output is written.
3. **Turn your workflows into skills.** For each unit, list the workflows done repeatedly and turn each one into a skill.
4. **Check the work.** A fresh checker reviews every draft, and each real mistake becomes a verification step or a rule inside the skill.
5. **Coordinate the team.** When a bot finishes its part, it hands the next step to the bot that owns it, and the manager keeps track of what’s stuck.
6. **Scale the team.** Add bots as the work grows, one at a time.
7. **Adapt as your work changes.** Once a week, review the team and put corrections into the skills.

## The principles

The principles live in one file, and every skill that builds, checks, or changes a bot reads it in full before its first step. Each section says when it applies.

| Principle | The rule |
| --- | --- |
| [Context isolation and traceability](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#context-isolation-and-traceability) | Give each unit of work its own bot, keep it in that unit's context, and make everything it does traceable. |
| [Context cascade](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#context-cascade) | Connect your systems of record first and get them right, because bad data at the source spreads to everything built on it. |
| [Make no assumptions](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#make-no-assumptions) | Ground every bot, skill, and answer in the person's real systems and examples. Look up any fact you have access to and give its source instead of asking, bring only preferences to the person, and ask before creating anything. |
| [Just-in-time context](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#just-in-time-context) | Open the template and the source right before the work, so nothing runs on stale facts. |
| [Procedure and perspective](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#procedure-and-perspective) | Give procedural work fixed steps and templates, and use the bot as a sparring partner for creative work. |
| [Dynamic orchestration](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#dynamic-orchestration) | The skill sets how the work splits and its limits, and the bot writes a short plan that fits that pattern to each run. |
| [Definition of good](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#definition-of-good) | Say what good looks like before you build, and put every correction back into the skill. |
| [Independent verification](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#independent-verification) | The agent that made an output doesn't grade it. A fresh check is the default last step. |
| [Human judgment](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#human-judgment) | Reading and drafting run freely. Anything that sends, changes a record, or can't be undone waits for your yes, and each yes covers one action. |
| [Handoffs and delegation](../../../explain-how-it-works/references/principles.md#handoffs-and-delegation) | The finishing bot messages the next owner directly and says what's done and what's next. |
