description: "Sharpen a task before starting: the expert approach, the goal beyond the goal, methods from other fields, and constraints that only box it in. Use for /sharpen."
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Before starting, ask how a top expert in the field would approach this task (their raw sources, their real methods, an outside check that the result is right), what the goal beyond this goal is, which other fields do this kind of work well, and whether the task is overspecified in ways that box the work in. Loosen constraints that don't serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger, without narrating the framing, and then do the work. Don't hand back a rewritten prompt.
