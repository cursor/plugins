name: sharpen
description: "Rewrite the user's prompt to be deeper, aim higher, and borrow from wider fields, then merge the best into one prompt. Use for /sharpen."
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Rewrite the user's prompt three ways: the way a top expert in the field would ask it (their raw sources, their real methods, an outside check that the result is right), aimed at the goal beyond this goal, and borrowing methods from other fields that do this kind of work well. Then merge what actually improves it into one short prompt, dropping any angle that adds nothing, and hand back only that prompt.
