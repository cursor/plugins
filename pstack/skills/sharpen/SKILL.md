Before starting, ask how a top expert in the field would approach this task (their raw sources, their real methods, an outside check that the result is right), what the goal beyond this goal is, and which other fields do this kind of work well. Fold what actually improves the work into your plan, drop any angle that adds nothing, and then do the work. Don't hand back a rewritten prompt.
